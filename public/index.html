Setup

  1. Install ffmpeg and make sure it's on your PATH.
  2. Install a virtual audio cable (e.g. VB-Audio Virtual Cable) — it creates a "CABLE Input" playback device and a "CABLE Output" recording device.
  3. On Windows, enable "Stereo Mix" under Sound Settings → Recording devices (or pick your virtual cable's own loopback device if your card doesn't have Stereo Mix).
  4. Pick the devices above and press Start.
  5. In Discord/WhatsApp, set your microphone input to "CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)".
